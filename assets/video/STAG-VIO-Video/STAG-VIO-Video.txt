dynamic environments remain a major challenge
for visual inertial orientation
moving pedestrians
vehicles and frequent occlusions
can easily corrupt feature tracking
and cause severe drift
to address this problem we propose STAG-VIO
a stabilized prompt geometry interface for robust
dynamic VIO
STAG-VIO consists of three stages
track to prompt
which stabilizes object prompts through uncertainty
Adaptive Multi object Tracking
prompt to mask
which generates category agnostic
dynamic masks with promptable segmentation
and mask to geometry
which filters dynamic features
and redistributes static constraints
for reliable VIO optimization
this significantly improves robustness
in highly dynamic scenes
where naive prompt
based segmentation often fails
our ablation study shows that prompt
stabilization is the most important component
reducing trajectory error by up to 83%
compared with seven baselines across 12 settings
STAG VIO achieves the lowest RMSE in eight settings
the advantage is most evident in highly dynamic scenes
where our method ranks first
in all three high dynamic cases
these results further confirm the robustness of STAG-VIO
under heavy motion and occlusion
we further validate the system on a real mobile robot
in complex urban traffic
with pedestrians cars
buses and motorcycles
in this real world experiment
STAG-VIO reduces RMSE by 57.9%
while producing trajectories
that align much more closely with the true path
